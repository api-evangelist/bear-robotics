Bear Robotics today announced it has signed a definitive agreement to acquire Kinisi Robotics , the Bristol-based humanoid and Physical AI startup behind the KR1 robot. The deal adds advanced manipulation intelligence to Bear's production robotics platform and marks a decisive step toward general-purpose robots that don't just move through the world, but act on it. It's a milestone the two teams have, in many ways, been building toward for years — and it completes the Physical AI platform Bear set out to build from the start.
