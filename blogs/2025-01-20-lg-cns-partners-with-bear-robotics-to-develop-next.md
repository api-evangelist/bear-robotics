Bear Robotics has taken another significant step forward in advancing the capabilities of autonomous mobile robots (AMRs). On January 17, 2025, LG CNS and Bear Robotics signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) to co-develop cutting-edge AMRs and integrated robot control solutions. This collaboration aims to revolutionize logistics operations with seamless automation and remote robot management systems.
