Redwood City, CA – May 5, 2025 – Bear Robotics, a global leader in robotics innovation for the hospitality industry, will showcase its latest advancements at the National Restaurant Association Show 2025, taking place May 17–20 at McCormick Place, Chicago. Attendees are invited to visit Booth #6217 in the North Hall to see how Bear Robotics is reshaping restaurant operations through intelligent automation. With rising labor costs and growing service demands, operators are turning to automation that delivers real-world results.
