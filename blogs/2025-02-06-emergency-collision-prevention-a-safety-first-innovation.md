Accidents happen when we least expect them, but with Bear Robotics’ Emergency Collision Prevention, our delivery robots ensure safety is always top of mind. This advanced feature actively tracks moving obstacles and applies emergency brakes if necessary to prevent collisions, creating a safer environment for both robots and people. Why emergency collision prevention matters Imagine a bustling restaurant or warehouse with people constantly moving around.
