The prevailing narrative suggests that the future of Artificial Intelligence is a two-horse race between the US and China. However, the latest analysis from the Harvard Business Review highlights a critical shift that is often overlooked: The next wave of innovation isn't just about Generative AI in the cloud-it is about Physical AI on the ground. HBR identifies Japan as a burgeoning powerhouse in this new landscape, citing Bear Robotics as a prime case study.
