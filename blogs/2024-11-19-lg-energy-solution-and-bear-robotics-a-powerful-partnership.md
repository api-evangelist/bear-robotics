LG Energy Solution is joining forces with Silicon Valley’s Bear Robotics to take robotics innovation to the next level. The two companies have signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU), making LG Energy Solution the exclusive supplier of cylindrical battery cells for Bear Robotics’ flagship service robots, including the Servi Plus and Carti 100 . The partnership will kick off next year and promises not just battery supply but also collaboration on new technologies.
